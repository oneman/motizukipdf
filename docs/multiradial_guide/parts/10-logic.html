10Logic: why “AND” and not “OR”論理：「かつ」と「または」

[EssLgc] is Mochizuki's 2022 paper on the logical structure of the theory. Its central claim is that the whole argument is a chain of logical AND relations (∧), and that reading any link of the chain as an OR (∨) makes the theory look either trivial or wrong. Example 2.4.5 gives a model with real numbers \(A, B > 0\) and \(0 \le \varepsilon \le 1\) that mirrors the three acts of §1.3:

actAND version (IUT)OR version
Θ-link\(N \overset{\text{def}}{=} -2B \;\wedge\; N \overset{\text{def}}{=} -A\)\(N = -2B \;\vee\; N = -A\)
multiradial representation\(N = -2A + \varepsilon \;\wedge\; N = -A\)\(N = -2A + \varepsilon \;\vee\; N = -A\)
final estimate\(-2A + \varepsilon = -A\), so \(A = \varepsilon \le 1\)nothing follows

Read the dictionary: \(N\) is the glued object, \(-A\) the q-pilot's log-volume, \(-2B\) the Θ-pilot's in its own universe (a dilation by 2 standing in for \(j^2\)), and \(-2A + \varepsilon\) the multiradial representation of the Θ-pilot, computed in the q-pilot's universe up to a small error \(\varepsilon\). In the AND reading the Θ-link is consistent precisely because \(A\) and \(B\) are allowed to be different numbers (two universes). The multiradial representation then re-expresses \(-2B\) in terms of \(A\), and both equations hold at once, which forces \(A \le 1\): a bound on the height.

In the OR reading the distinct \(A, B\) look superfluous, one is tempted to set \(A = B\) “for the sake of simplicity”, at which point the AND version of the Θ-link becomes self-contradictory (\(-2A = -A\)) and the passage to the final estimate “looks as if one erroneously replaced the ‘∨’ […] by an ‘∧’ without any mathematical justification whatsoever.” Mochizuki attributes the 2018 objection (§12) to this chain, which he calls the “redundant copies school” (RCS).

10.1How the AND survives the algorithm

[EssLgc] §3.6–§3.10 then tracks the ∧ through every step of Figure 6.1. Each step either keeps it (the descents (Stp2), (Stp3) replace data by equivalent algorithms on weaker data; (Stp5), (Stp6) do not change the possible value-group portions), or enlarges a set of possibilities, which only adds ∨'s inside one side and never across the Θ-link (\(\dot\vee\), “(ExtInd1)”). The closed loop of (Stp7) brings both sides into one copy of \(\mathbb{R}\), where the ∧ becomes an inclusion of a number in a half-line. The danger [EssLgc] warns about most is (AOC3): wanting a proof that “taking log-volumes of pilot objects in the domain and codomain of the Θ-link yields the same real number”. In the AND reading there is no such statement to prove; the two log-volumes live in different universes until the very last step.

Otaku corner · 二股

In a harem romcom the protagonist is with heroine A or heroine B, and the whole season is about which. IUT is the rare series in which the protagonist is, for structural reasons, with A and B at once, because they live in different universes and never meet. Fans who insist “it has to be one of them” (the RCS reading) conclude the plot makes no sense. Fans who accept the AND find the ending forced: the only consistent epilogue is the one where the protagonist's height is bounded. Opinions on whether this ending was earned are in §12.
