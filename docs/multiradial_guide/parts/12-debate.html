Neutral ground

This section reports what the participants have written, in their own words where possible, and maps each position onto the steps of this guide. It takes no side. The toolkit in this repository implements the statements of the theory as data types and bookkeeping; it cannot settle whether Corollary 3.12 is proved.

Almost all of the disagreement about IUT is concentrated on exactly the material of this guide: the passage from Theorem 3.11 to Corollary 3.12. Nobody seriously disputes the earlier anabelian results the theory consumes (Mochizuki's own 2026 report says the material of “Stages 3–5” has “never been a source of confusion/debate”). 12.1The disagreement, mapped onto the algorithm

Put in the vocabulary of this guide, the positions differ on one question: at step (xi), are the two routes of Figure 8.1 really computing the same thing, in the same copy of \(\mathbb{R}\), with the blur on the correct side?

  • Mochizuki's reading. Yes, by construction: SHE and IPL say the output is expressible in column 1's ring structure and is linked to the fixed input; the closed loop of (Stp7) places both log-volumes in one container; everything else is an AND that was never broken. Asking for a separate proof that the two pilots' log-volumes agree is the error (AOC3).
  • The Scholze–Stix reading. If the copies can be identified (they argue nothing is lost by doing so), the argument reduces to a comparison in which the indeterminacies must absorb the factor \(j^2\), and then the inequality has no content. They locate the gap in the proof of Corollary 3.12.
  • Joshi's reading. The copies cannot be naively identified (agreeing with Mochizuki against Scholze–Stix), but a needed property of the construction is not proved in [IUTchIII], so Theorem 3.11 itself is incomplete.
  • LANA's reading, as of July 2026. No verdict; the formalization has reached a specific compatibility involving (Ind1)–(Ind3) that it has not yet been able to reconstruct from the papers.

What all four have in common is that the argument has been narrowed to a small part of the theory, the part this guide covers, and that formalization is now the route by which it will be tested. The toolkit's Lean port in lean/ formalizes the data model and the arithmetic of the procession (§9.2), not the theorem.

Otaku corner · 考察班

Every big series has a 考察班 (kōsatsu-han), the fan research squad that rewatches episodes frame by frame. IUT's squad is unusually distinguished, and for fourteen years it has been rewatching the same scene: the ten minutes between Theorem 3.11 and Corollary 3.12. The 2026 twist is that the squad now includes a proof assistant, which does not get tired, does not take sides, and does not accept “it is obvious from the definitions” as a frame. As of this writing, the season finale has been delayed.
