3Multiradiality: the one definition you need多輻性とは

The word “multiradial” comes from a picture of a wheel. The hub is something shared (the coric data, from “core”), and the spokes are the different richer structures that sit over it (the radial data). The question is whether the hub can be turned without breaking the spokes.

Definition · [IUTchII] Example 1.7; [Alien] §3.1 (ii)–(iii)

A radial environment is a triple: a type of radial data \(\mathcal{R}\), a type of coric data \(\mathcal{C}\), and a functorial radial algorithm \(\mathcal{R} \to \mathcal{C}\) that forgets the finer structure. It is multiradial if the resulting functor is full: every isomorphism between the coric data underlying two radial data lifts to an isomorphism of the radial data. It is uniradial otherwise.

Fullness has a vivid consequence, the switching property [Alien, §3.1 (iii)]: take two radial data \(R_1, R_2\) together with an isomorphism \(\alpha\) between their coric data. Swapping the roles of \(R_1\) and \(R_2\) (and replacing \(\alpha\) by \(\alpha^{-1}\)) gives an isomorphic configuration. Multiradiality is equivalent to every such pair being isomorphic to a “diagonal” pair \((R, R, \mathrm{id})\). In words: once you only look through the hub, you cannot tell which spoke is which.

3.1The prototype: complex structures on a real plane

Note what just happened. Making something multiradial is easy if you are willing to throw information away: replace your structure by the set of all structures it could be. The tautological recipe even has a name, multiradialization [IUTchII, Ex. 1.7 (ii)]. The art of Theorem 3.11 is to throw away exactly enough. The blurred description must still determine the one number we care about, the log-volume, up to an error we can bound. Everything in §6 and §7 is about that balance.

The whole game in one sentence

Find a description of the Θ-pilot that is (1) multiradial, so that the person on the other side of the Θ-link can read it, and (2) still informative about log-volumes, so that reading it tells them something. Either one alone is trivial. Both together is Theorem 3.11.

3.2Multiradiality as parallel transport

Mochizuki's favourite way to think about multiradiality is through connections [IUTchII, Rem. 1.7.1; Alien, §3.1 (iv)]. Grothendieck's definition of a connection on a bundle \(E\) over \(X/S\) is an isomorphism between the fibres of \(E\) at two infinitesimally close points of \(X\) lying over the same point of \(S\). Read this as: two “radial” fibres over the same “coric” base point, and a rule for transporting one to the other. Fullness of the radial functor says such transports exist along every path in the coric data; the leftover choice in the lift is a kind of monodromy. In IUT, that monodromy is precisely the indeterminacies (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3).

The analogy goes further. [Alien] §3.1 (v) rewrites the classical proof of the geometric Szpiro inequality via the Kodaira–Spencer map in these terms: a rank-two bundle \(\mathcal{E}\) with a logarithmic connection serves as a container for the line bundle \(\omega_E \subseteq \mathcal{E}\). Transport \(\omega_E\) from one factor of \(S\times S\) to the other through the container and measure how far it moves; the discrepancy is the Kodaira–Spencer map, and its non-vanishing bounds \(\deg \omega_E\). The multiradial representation is the arithmetic version: tensor packets of log-shells are the container, the Θ-pilot is the line bundle, and the blur is the discrepancy.

Analogy · karaoke key change

The backing track is coric: everyone in the karaoke box hears the same one. The singer's voice is radial. A song is “uniradial” if it only works in the original key. It is “multiradial” if every transposition of the backing track (the machine's ♯/♭ buttons, which act on the coric data) can be matched by the singer. The tautological way to be multiradial is to hum tunelessly, which matches every key and says nothing. The Theorem 3.11 way is to sing so that the loudness of the performance (the log-volume) is the same in every key, even if you cannot say which key you are in.

Otaku corner · 推し

Fandom has its own radial environment. The coric data is the anime as broadcast; the radial data is each fan's interpretation. An interpretation is “multiradial” if it survives every re-edit of the broadcast: director's cut, Blu-ray fixes to the animation, the movie recap. Most fan theories are uniradial and die with the Blu-ray release. The rare multiradial ones are vague enough to survive and specific enough to predict the box-office numbers. Theorem 3.11 is the fan theory that predicts the box office.
