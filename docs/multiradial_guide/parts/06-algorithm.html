6.1Step 0: the input is a prime-strip, and it is held fixed

A crucial reinterpretation comes first [IUTchIII, Rem. 3.11.1 (ii)]. Theorem 3.11 can be regarded as an algorithm “whose input data is an F×μ-prime-strip […] and whose functoriality is with respect to arbitrary isomorphisms of the F×μ-prime-strips that appear as input data”. Because the Θ-link identifies the F×μ-prime-strip of the Θ-pilot on the left with that of the q-pilot on the right, the input can be taken to be the q-pilot's prime-strip at \((1,0)\), together with the ring structure of column 1 that rigidifies it. That input is then “coric”: it “remains invariant”/“may be regarded as being held fixed” throughout the algorithm. Everything else moves; the q-pilot stays put. This is what makes the final comparison possible.

Step 1 (Stp1) · Kummer theory and Galois evaluationInd3
in: the Frobenius-like Θ-pilot at \((0,0)\) · out: étale-like theta values and number fields over \((0,\circ)\) · [IUTchIII] Thm 3.11 (ii); [EssLgc] §3.10 (Stp1)

This is the step that occupies most of [IUTchI–III]. Its job is to exhibit the Frobenius-like Θ-pilot at \((0,0)\) “as one possibility within a collection of possibilities […] constructed via anabelian algorithms from holomorphic […] étale-like data” [EssLgc, §3.10 (Stp1)]. Three machines run in parallel:

  • Theta values. The étale theta function on the Tate curve at each \(v\in\mathbb{V}^{\mathrm{bad}}\) is reconstructed from the tempered fundamental group. Its Kummer theory runs through mono-theta environments, whose cyclotome is rigid (“mono-theta-theoretic cyclotomic rigidity”, [EtTh]). Evaluating it at the \(l\)-torsion points labelled \(j\in|\mathbb{F}_l|\) (Galois evaluation, synchronized across labels by the \(\mathbb{F}_l^{\rtimes\pm}\)-symmetry) produces the values \(\underline{q}^{\,j^2}\) as étale-like monoids [IUTchII, Cor. 2.5, 3.5].
  • Number fields. Kummer theory of κ-coric rational functions on the curve reconstructs copies \((F_{\mathrm{mod}}^\times)_j\) of the multiplicative group of the field of moduli, one per label, related by the \(\mathbb{F}_l^{*}\)-symmetry [IUTchI, Ex. 5.1; IUTchII, Cor. 4.8].
  • The log-Kummer correspondence. These Kummer isomorphisms exist at every node \((0,m)\) of the column, and Theorem 3.11 (ii) says they are compatible with the log-links, except that the images of the local units only satisfy inclusions: this is (Ind3). A non-interference property keeps the damage small: the theta values and \(F_{\mathrm{mod}}^\times\) meet the local units only in roots of unity [IUTchIII, Prop. 3.5 (ii)(c), 3.10 (ii)].

Step 2 (Stp2) · descend to the Galois groupsInd1
in: holomorphic étale-like data over \((0,\circ)\) · out: the same, rebuilt from the D⊢-prime-strips \(\{G_v\}\) alone · [IUTchIII] Thm 3.11 (i); [EssLgc] §3.10 (Stp2)

The constructions of Step 1 used the full arithmetic fundamental groups \(\Pi_v\). Step 2 observes that they “in fact descend to […] algorithms that only require as input data the weaker data” of the mono-analytic Galois groups \(G_v\). This is essential because \(G_v\) is what the Θ-link shares and \(\Pi_v\) is not. The data are arranged along the procession \(\{\mathcal{D}_0\} \to \{\mathcal{D}_0,\mathcal{D}_1\}\to\dots\to\{\mathcal{D}_0,\dots,\mathcal{D}_{l^*}\}\): row \(j\) of Figure 6.2 is built over the capsule \(\{\mathcal{D}_0,\dots,\mathcal{D}_j\}\) and consists of

  1. the tensor packet \(\mathcal{I}^{\mathbb{Q}}(\mathbb{S}^\pm_{j+1})\): the tensor product of the \(j+1\) log-shells, one per label, at each place \(v_{\mathbb{Q}}\) of \(\mathbb{Q}\) (a direct sum over the places \(v\) above \(v_{\mathbb{Q}}\));
  2. at \(v\in\mathbb{V}^{\mathrm{bad}}\), the splitting monoid \(\Psi^\perp_{\mathrm{LGP}}\), generated by \(\underline{q}^{\,j^2}\), acting multiplicatively on the packets;
  3. the number field \(M_{\mathrm{mod}}(\dots)_j \subseteq \mathcal{I}^{\mathbb{Q}}(\mathbb{S}^\pm_{j+1})\) with its global Frobenioids, whose global degrees are computed by log-volumes.

Working only with \(G_v\) introduces (Ind1): the automorphisms of the procession. A capsule of \(j+1\) unordered prime-strips has \((j+1)!\) automorphisms; the procession as a whole has \(\prod_j (j+1)! = {{ind1}}\) for \(l = {{l}}\) (and \({{ind1big}}\) for \(l = 11\)). It also includes the automorphisms of each abstract \(G_v\), which do not preserve the ring structure of the field it came from. Processions exist precisely to keep this blur small: without them the labels could be permuted in \((l^\pm)^{l^\pm}\) ways, with them only \(l^\pm!\).

Step 3 (Stp3) · descend to the F×μ-prime-stripInd2
in: data over \(\{G_v\}\) · out: data over \(\{G_v \curvearrowright \mathcal{O}^{\times\mu}_v\}\), which the Θ-link glues to \((1,0)\) · [IUTchIII] Thm 3.11 (i), (iii); [EssLgc] §3.10 (Stp3)

One more descent: the log-shells are now built “as submonoids of \(\mathcal{O}^{\times\mu}_{F_v}\), as opposed to subquotients of \(G_v\)”, i.e. from the mono-analytic Frobenius-like data of the F×μ-prime-strip. This is the data the Θ-link actually glues: the unit portion of \({}^{0,0}\mathcal{F}^{\times\mu}_{\mathrm{LGP}} \xrightarrow{\sim} {}^{1,0}\mathcal{F}^{\times\mu}\). Identifying Frobenius-like and étale-like units costs (Ind2): independent copies of the isometry group \(\mathrm{Ism}\) of \(\mathcal{O}^{\times\mu}\) act on each direct summand of each factor of each tensor packet (for archimedean places, the automorphisms of order 2). The bi-coricity of the units (Theorem 1.5 (iii), (iv)) is what lets the mono-analytic log-shells serve as “multiradial mono-analytic containers” for the value-group data of column 0 [IUTchIII, proof of Cor. 3.12, Step (v)].

Why are the theta values not destroyed by (Ind1) and (Ind2)? Because their cyclotomes come from mono-theta environments and from \(F_{\mathrm{mod}}\), which are insulated from the automorphisms of \(\mathcal{F}^{\times\mu}\) [IUTchIII, proof of Cor. 3.12, Step (ii), Fig. 3.6]. This is the single most important technical fact behind the theorem, and it is why the étale theta function, rather than any other function, sits at the centre of IUT.

6.2The output

Write \({}^{n,\circ}\mathfrak{R}^{\mathrm{LGP}}\) for the data (a), (b), (c) regarded up to (Ind1) and (Ind2) (with (Ind3) recording how the Frobenius-like data at each \((n,m)\) sit inside). Theorem 3.11 (i) says this can be constructed by an algorithm in the procession of D⊢-prime-strips, functorially; the permutation symmetries of the étale-picture give compatible isomorphisms \({}^{n,\circ}\mathfrak{R}^{\mathrm{LGP}} \xrightarrow{\sim} {}^{n',\circ}\mathfrak{R}^{\mathrm{LGP}}\) for all \(n, n'\). In other words, the representation built over column 0 is the same as the one built over column 1. Theorem 3.11 (iii) adds that all of this is compatible with the Θ×μ_LGP-link: the constructions are “stabilized/equivariant/functorial with respect to arbitrary automorphisms of the domain and codomain” of the horizontal arrows.

6.3The four properties: IPL, SHE, APT, HIS

Remark 3.11.1 names four properties of the output that the corollary uses. They are worth learning as a set, because they are what the rest of the argument actually consumes.

IPL · input prime-strip linkThe output is linked, by full poly-isomorphisms of prime-strips produced by the algorithm itself, to the input: the q-pilot prime-strip with its column-1 ring structure. Every intermediate prime-strip could itself serve as input.Rem 3.11.1 (iii)
SHE · simultaneous holomorphic expressibilityThe construction and its output are “simultaneously valid/executable/well-defined” relative to both the ring structure of the domain (where the Θ-pilot lives) and that of the codomain (where the input lives).Rem 3.11.1 (iii)

“[T]heta functions/theta values may be regarded as HIS of the construction algorithm of Theorem 3.11 — somewhat like the internal structure of the CPU or operating system of a computer! — i.e., internal structures whose technical details are […] irrelevant or uninteresting from the point of view of the ‘end user’ […]. Otaku corner · シンクロ率

Mono-theta-theoretic cyclotomic rigidity is the IUT equivalent of a sync ratio that never drops. Every other cyclotome in the theory can be rescaled by \(\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}^\times\) when you look at it from another universe, the way a pilot's synchronization wavers under stress. The cyclotome of the mono-theta environment cannot: its rigidity comes from the quadratic structure of theta-group commutators [IUTchIII, Rem. 2.1.1; Alien, §3.8 (9gau)], so it locks at one value whatever the universe. That single unflappable pilot is what lets the theta values cross the Θ-link intact.
