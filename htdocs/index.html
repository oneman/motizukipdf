Inter-universal Teichmüller theory: the data types, then the processes that use them

Part I shows every data type of IUT I–IV as a diagram (initial Θ-data, label sets, prime-strips, Hodge theaters, links, the log-theta-lattice, log-shells, indeterminacies, the multiradial representation, species). Part II walks step by step through the processes that act on them, following [Alien] (the Gaussian-integral analogy, mono-anabelian transport, the Θ-link) and [EssLgc] (the chain of logical AND relations (Stp1)–(Stp8), ∧ versus ∨). Part III is the interactive explorer: generate initial Θ-data, build the lattice, validate everything.
